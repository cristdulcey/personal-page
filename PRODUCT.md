# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Audience**: CTOs, VPs of Engineering, enterprise technical founders, and decision-makers evaluating senior architectural consulting, high-concurrency systems design, or executive engineering advisory.
- **Secondary Audience**: Senior engineers, architects, and technical peers seeking rigorous reference material, deep architectural insights, and proven distributed systems patterns.

## Product Purpose

Establish Cristian Dulcey as an authoritative, tier-one software and distributed systems architect. The product exists to demonstrate sovereign-scale engineering capabilities through verified case studies and technical writing, converting high-value peer and executive interest into high-impact advisory and strategic collaboration. Success means zero ambiguity regarding technical depth, executive credibility, and engineering caliber.

## Positioning

Architectural execution backed by verifiable sovereign and nation-state scale deployments (Chivo Wallet core banking/ledger infrastructure for 4M+ citizens under legal-tender SLA; Proyecto Fénix 300+ microservices on Azure with 100K+ concurrent requests). The portfolio rejects superficial freelancer tropes, cosmetic AI templates, and generic claims in favor of concrete engineering evidence, architectural schematics, and production metrics.

## Operating Context

Evaluators review this portfolio asynchronously on desktop and mobile devices—often during executive searches, advisory board assessments, conference reviews, or technical due diligence. The experience must load instantly, maintain strict bilingual consistency (ES/EN), and communicate authority within seconds of scanning.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Bilingual Surface**: Full English (`/en/`) and Spanish (`/`) localization across all routes and technical essays.
- **Evidence-Driven Case Studies**: Dedicated in-depth project pages (`/projects/{slug}` and `/en/projects/{slug}`) containing quantifiable metrics, system topology schematics, and concrete decisions.
- **Interactive Telemetry & Schematics**: Technical components demonstrating architecture topologies and systems telemetry without decorative gimmicks.
- **Zero AI-Template Anti-Patterns**: Absolute bans on gradient text, dark chromatic halos, side-tab borders, pulsing decorative rings, and micro-donation widgets.
- **Performance & Standards**: Static generation via Astro, responsive CSS, WCAG AAA/AA contrast compliance, zero JavaScript bloat.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Cristian Dulcey
- **Title**: Lead Systems Architect & Infrastructure Engineer (GDE & MVP)
- **Voice**: Authoritative, restrained, pedagogical, and rigorous. Direct and evidence-based.
- **Visual Restraint**: High-contrast typography (`Public Sans`), architectural geometry, and blueprint/editorial layouts over decorative visual fluff.

- **Proyecto Fénix Case Study**: `src/content/projects/es/fenix.md` and `src/content/projects/en/fenix.md` (300+ microservices, Azure Kubernetes, 100K+ concurrent requests, 99.99% SLA).

## Product Principles

1. **Evidence Precedes Claims**: Every claim of capability must link to a real architecture, concrete metric, or tangible technical post-mortem.
2. **Clarity and Restraint over Decoration**: Interfaces must feel like an institutional engineering briefing, not an agency marketing showcase.
3. **Institutional Durability**: Information architecture, typography, and contrast must be robust, accessible, and timeless across all viewports.
4. **Peer-Level Respect**: Treat the reader as an intelligent technical peer or executive; avoid patronizing oversimplifications.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA / AAA standards (text contrast > 7:1 for body and titles, interactive targets >= 44x44px).
- Respect for reduced motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
- Clear semantic HTML structure with accessible landmarks, keyboard navigation, and bilingual metadata.
